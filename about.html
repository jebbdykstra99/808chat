All things Hawaii. Local, not brochure.

808chat is a room for people who live here, grew up here, or already have skin in the islands — not a vacation planner with a chat bolted on.

We talk weather when the trades shift, breaks when the swell shows, island stuff when it matters on your side of the channel. Room Briefs pull from NWS Honolulu.

What this is not

Not a booking engine. Not a package board. Not a “top 10 must-do” carousel.

What you’ll find

Island hubs: Oʻahu, Maui, Kauaʻi, Big Island. Breaks. Talk story.

808 isn’t only the islands. It’s also an area code with a birthday on 8/8, a drum machine that rewired popular music, a lucky stack of eights in Chinese folk talk, a number people read as abundance or new beginnings. Hawaii is the spine. The other meanings get their own seats — labeled so FACT and folk don’t get confused.

Guest can browse. Sign in for a handle.
